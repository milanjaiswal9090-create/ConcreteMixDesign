Technical Calculation Methodology

Engineering principles, deterministic algorithms, and IS code boundary rules powering ConcreteMix AI.

1. Core Design Philosophy: Deterministic vs Generative

ConcreteMix AI separates natural-language understanding and reference retrieval from numerical engineering calculations. No LLM calculates batch weights or water-cement ratios.

Instead, problems are converted into typed data structures that are processed by our deterministic mathematical implementation of IS 10262:2019 and verified against IS 456:2000 Table 5.

2. Target Mean Compressive Strength (Clause 4.2)

The target mean compressive strength at 28 days (\(f'_{ck}\)) is governed by:

f'ck = max( fck + 1.65 × s, fck + X )

Where s is the assumed standard deviation (3.5 for M10-M15, 4.0 for M20-M25, 5.0 for M30-M60) and X is the addition factor based on concrete grade (5.0, 5.5, 6.5, or 8.0 N/mm²).

3. Selection of Water-Cement Ratio and Durability Bounds

The initial w/c ratio is obtained from 28-day strength curves (OPC 33, 43, 53 grade) and checked against the maximum permissible limit specified in IS 456:2000 Table 5 for the given exposure condition. The lower (stricter) value is adopted.

4. Absolute Volume Method & 100% Volume Yield Check

Every cubic metre (1.0 m³) of compacted concrete satisfies the absolute volume summation equation:

V_cement + V_SCM + V_water + V_admixture + V_air + V_coarse_agg + V_fine_agg = 1.000 m³

Entrapped air volume is accounted for based on aggregate size (0.8% for 40 mm, 1.0% for 20 mm, 1.5% for 10 mm).
