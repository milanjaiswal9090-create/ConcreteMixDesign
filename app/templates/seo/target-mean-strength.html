Target Mean Compressive Strength in IS 10262

Detailed explanation of Clause 4.2, assumed standard deviation (s), and the addition factor (X).

Understanding the Target Strength Formula

In IS 10262:2019, the target mean compressive strength (\(f'_{ck}\)) ensures that not more than 5% of test results fall below characteristic strength (\(f_{ck}\)). Clause 4.2 mandates:

f'ck = max( fck + 1.65 × s, fck + X )

Where:

  • s: Standard deviation in N/mm² (Table 1: 3.5 for M10/M15, 4.0 for M20/M25, 5.0 for M30-M60).
  • X: Factor based on grade (Table 2: 5.0 for M10-M15, 5.5 for M20-M25, 6.5 for M30-M45, 8.0 for M50-M60).
