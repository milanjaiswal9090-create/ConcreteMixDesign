IS 10262:2019 Concrete Mix Proportioning Guide

A technical walkthrough of India's benchmark standard for proportioning normal and high-strength concrete mixes.

1. Introduction to the 2019 Revision

Bureau of Indian Standards published IS 10262:2019 to supersede the 2009 edition. The revised standard includes provisions for high-strength concrete (M65-M100), self-compacting concrete (SCC), mass concrete, and systematic handling of mineral admixtures (fly ash, GGBS, silica fume, and metakaolin).

2. Key Steps in the Design Procedure

  1. Design Stipulations: Establish characteristic compressive strength (\(f_{ck}\)), exposure condition, workability (slump), placing method (pumped vs tremie), and quality control level.
  2. Target Mean Strength: Calculate \(f'_{ck} = \max(f_{ck} + 1.65s, f_{ck} + X)\).
  3. Selection of Free Water-Cement Ratio: Derive preliminary w/c from strength curves and limit according to IS 456 Table 5 durability requirements.
  4. Selection of Water Content: Base water requirement on nominal aggregate size (10, 20, 40 mm) with +3% adjustments per 25 mm slump above 50 mm, and water reduction for chemical admixtures.
  5. Calculation of Cementitious Content: Cementitious = Water / (w/c), verified against IS 456 minimum cement content and 450 kg/m³ maximum limit.
  6. Aggregate Proportioning: Estimate coarse aggregate volume fraction per unit volume of total aggregate based on fine aggregate grading zone (Zones I to IV) and adopted w/c ratio.
  7. Absolute Volume Calculations: Sum component volumes to 1.0 m³ to compute precise batch masses.
