Water-Cement Ratio Selection & Durability Limits

Balancing target compressive strength curves with IS 456:2000 environmental durability boundaries.

Durability Governing vs Strength Governing

The water-cement ratio is chosen by evaluating two independent criteria:

  1. Strength Criterion: The w/c ratio required to achieve the target mean strength (\(f'_{ck}\)) based on the cement grade (OPC 33, 43, 53) strength curve.
  2. Durability Criterion: The maximum allowable free water-cement ratio specified in IS 456:2000 Table 5 for the given exposure condition (0.55 for Mild, 0.50 for Moderate, 0.45 for Severe, 0.45 for Very Severe, 0.40 for Extreme in RCC).

The adopted w/c ratio is the lower of the two values.
