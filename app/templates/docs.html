Documentation & Frequently Asked Questions

Guidance on using the platform, input boundaries, and engineering interpretations.

Does this mix design platform use an external LLM API?

No. There is zero runtime dependence on external generative LLM APIs (OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, etc.). Parameter parsing uses deterministic regex matchers, RAG retrieval uses hybrid BM25 and local TF-IDF embeddings, and calculations use the verified IS 10262 Python algorithm.

Can calculated proportions be used directly on site?

No mathematical proportioning algorithm replaces physical laboratory testing. IS 10262:2019 mix designs produce starting target proportions that must be verified by laboratory trial mixes to confirm actual workability, 7-day strength, and 28-day compressive strength with locally sourced batch materials.

How are field moisture and water absorption handled?

Mix designs calculate bone-dry or saturated-surface-dry (SSD) quantities. When aggregates in the yard possess free surface moisture or water absorption deficiencies, the mixing water and aggregate batch weights are automatically adjusted in the "Site Adjusted Field Batching" summary.

What aggregate sizes and grading zones are supported?

Standard nominal maximum aggregate sizes of 10 mm, 20 mm, and 40 mm are supported. Fine aggregate grading Zones I, II, III, and IV (as per IS 383:2016) are fully mapped to coarse aggregate volume fractions.
