Standard Specification

IS 10262:2019

Guidelines for concrete mix proportioning. Implements target mean compressive strength (Clause 4.2), water content selection for 10/20/40 mm aggregates, slump corrections (+3% per 25 mm), coarse aggregate volume ratios across Zones I-IV, and SCM handling.

Durability Specification

IS 456:2000 (Table 5)

Code of practice for plain and reinforced concrete. Governs maximum free water-cement ratios (0.55 down to 0.40), minimum cementitious contents (up to 360 kg/m³), and minimum concrete grades across Mild, Moderate, Severe, Very Severe, and Extreme environments.
